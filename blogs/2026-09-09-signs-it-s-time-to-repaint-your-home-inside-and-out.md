Learn the signs it’s time to repaint your home, including peeling paint, fading, cracking, moisture damage, and how paint protects your home inside and out. Paint does far more than improve how your home looks—it serves as a critical protective barrier. Over time, exposure to weather, moisture, and everyday wear causes paint to break down.
